# SQ

SQ (short for Squirrel) is a lightweight, self-hosted project tracker for
people with seventeen projects and one attention span. It runs as a single
container on your own hardware, keeps everything in one folder, and comes
with a built-in conscience: it remembers every project you started and
nags you, kindly, before you chase the next shiny thing.

## What you get

- Drag-and-drop boards with cards, subtasks that nest under their parent,
  checklists, labels, priorities, due dates, recurrence, and per-column
  work-in-progress limits.
- A Today's Tasks list, shared or personal, that feeds wall displays: a
  tap-friendly kiosk for tablets and Raspberry Pis, compact JSON for
  ESP32 screens, and a pop-up view for Android TVs.
- Attachments on any card with previews for office files, images, PDF,
  video, audio, and 3D models rendered right in the page.
- Optional AI assist (Gemini, Claude, OpenAI, or local Ollama) that
  breaks cards into subtasks, researches approaches, plans your day, and
  packages any card as a prompt for an outside AI.
- Multiple users with three simple roles, Home Assistant integration,
  and a dashboard that shows what Claude Code is doing across your
  development machines.

## Where to start

Head to [Getting started](getting-started.md) to bring up your own
instance in a couple of minutes, then take the tour through
[Boards and cards](boards.md).

SQ is free, open source, and self-hosted; your projects never leave your
network unless you send them somewhere. The squirrel stays home.
